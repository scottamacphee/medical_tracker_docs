Privacy Policy for Wear Time

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

Wear Time ("the App") is designed to help users track their medical device wear time. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third parties.

Data Storage

All information you enter into the App — including wear session times, daily goals, and notes — is stored locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not uploaded to any server
  • Is not accessible to the developer
  • Is not shared with any third party

Data You Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete this data at any time by deleting the app
  • No account creation or login is required
  • No internet connection is required to use core features

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Medical Disclaimer: This App is intended for personal tracking purposes only and is not a certified medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult your healthcare provider regarding your medical device usage and treatment plan.
